LDetect-lite is a re-implementation of [LDetect](https://bitbucket.org/nygcresearch/ldetect) that performs fast calculation of approximately independent linkage disequilibrium blocks from genotype data. See the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/adamyhe/LDetect-lite) for code and documentation.

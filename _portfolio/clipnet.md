CLIPNET is an ensemble of deep learning models that predict transcription initiation from DNA sequence at single nucleotide resolution, revealing the regulatory syntax of core promoters. See the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Danko-Lab/clipnet) for code, pretrained models, and documentation.
